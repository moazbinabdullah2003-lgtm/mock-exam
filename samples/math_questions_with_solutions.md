**Q1.** 🟢 The number of points of intersection of the curve $f(x)=1+x^3$ with the $x$-axis is-

**(a)** $0$ \
**(b)** $1$ \
**(c)** $2$ \
**(d)** $3$

**Answer: (b)**

**Solution:**
1. Intersection with the $x$-axis means $f(x)=0$, so $1+x^3=0$.
2. $x^3=-1$, and the only real cube root of $-1$ is $x=-1$.
3. So the curve cuts the $x$-axis at exactly **1 point**.

**Q2.** 🟢 If $z=x+iy$, then $|z-5|+|z+5|=16$ represents-

**(a)** a circle \
**(b)** a parabola \
**(c)** a hyperbola \
**(d)** an ellipse

**Answer: (d)**

**Solution:**
1. $|z-5|$ is the distance of $z$ from $(5,0)$ and $|z+5|$ is the distance from $(-5,0)$.
2. The sum of the distances from two fixed points (foci) is constant, $16$.
3. The distance between the foci is $10$, and $16>10$, so this is an **ellipse**.

**Q3.** 🟡 For what values of $k$ will the roots of the equation $(k^2-3)x^2+3kx+3k+1=0$ be reciprocals of each other?

**(a)** $4,\ 1$ \
**(b)** $4,\ -1$ \
**(c)** $-4,\ 1$ \
**(d)** $6,\ -1$

**Answer: (b)**

**Solution:**
1. Roots are reciprocals, so their product is $1$.
2. Product of roots $=\dfrac{3k+1}{k^2-3}=1$.
3. $3k+1=k^2-3 \Rightarrow k^2-3k-4=0$.
4. $(k-4)(k+1)=0 \Rightarrow k=4$ or $k=-1$.

**Q4.** 🟢 If one root of a quadratic equation is $\dfrac{1}{3-i\sqrt{2}}$, which of the following is the other root?

**(a)** $\dfrac{3}{11}-i\,\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{11}$ \
**(b)** $\dfrac{3}{11}+i\,\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{11}$ \
**(c)** $\dfrac{3i}{11}-\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{11}$ \
**(d)** $\dfrac{3i}{11}+\dfrac{\sqrt{2}}{11}$

**Answer: (a)**

**Solution:**
1. Rationalise: $\dfrac{1}{3-i\sqrt2}\times\dfrac{3+i\sqrt2}{3+i\sqrt2}=\dfrac{3+i\sqrt2}{9+2}=\dfrac{3}{11}+i\dfrac{\sqrt2}{11}$.
2. For a quadratic with real coefficients, complex roots come in conjugate pairs.
3. The other root is the conjugate: $\dfrac{3}{11}-i\dfrac{\sqrt2}{11}$.

**Q5.** 🟡 If $\dfrac{1}{a+i}=\dfrac{i}{a-i}$, then what is the value of $a$?

2. For $n\ge3$, all four terms of $(1+x)^3$ contribute a coefficient $1$ times that.
3. Sum $=1+3+3+1=8$.

**Q46.** 🔴 The coefficient of $x^7$ in the expansion of $(1+x)^7(1-x)^8$ is-

**(a)** $15$ \
**(b)** $25$ \
**(c)** $30$ \
**(d)** $35$

**Answer: (d)**

**Solution:**

2. $(1-x^2)^7$ has only even powers. For $x^7$ we need $(-x)\times x^6$.
3. Coefficient of $x^6$ in $(1-x^2)^7$ is ${}^7C_3(-1)^3=-35$.
4. So the coefficient of $x^7$ is $(-1)(-35)=35$.

**Q48.** 🔴 The roots of the equation $2x^2-7x+5=0$ are $\alpha$ and $\beta$; the roots of the equation $x^2-4x+3=0$ are $\beta$ and $\gamma$. Then $(\gamma+\alpha):(\gamma-\alpha)=\ ?$

**(b)** $5:6$ \
**(c)** $11:1$ \
**(d)** $1:6$

**Solution:**

2. $x^2-4x+3=(x-1)(x-3)$, so the roots are $1$ and $3$.

**Q49.** 🔴 If $\omega^3=1$, then $(1+\omega-\omega^2)(\omega+\omega^2-1)(\omega^2+1-\omega)=\ ?$

**(a)** $0$ \
**(b)** $8$ \
**(c)** $-8$ \
**(d)** $1$

**Answer: (c)**

**Solution:**
1. Take $\omega$ as the complex cube root, so $1+\omega+\omega^2=0$.
2. $1+\omega-\omega^2=-\omega^2-\omega^2=-2\omega^2$.
3. $\omega+\omega^2-1=-1-1=-2$.
4. $\omega^2+1-\omega=-\omega-\omega=-2\omega$.
5. Product $=(-2\omega^2)(-2)(-2\omega)=-8\omega^3=-8$.

**Q50.** 🔴 If $|x-1|<\dfrac{1}{10}$, which of the following is true?
